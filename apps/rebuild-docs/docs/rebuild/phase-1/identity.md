## Positioning

PoopPOS is a lightweight, open-source-based POS system designed for Nicaraguan businesses that need simple, reliable sales tracking without expensive hardware or complex infrastructure.

**Key differentiators:**
- Low hardware requirements (works on old/low-end computers)
- Intermittent internet support
- Spanish-first interface
- Affordable SaaS pricing in Córdobas
- Local support via WhatsApp

## Pricing tiers

| Plan | Target | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Básico | Small business, single POS | Sales, expenses, simple inventory, 1 user, WhatsApp support |
| Estándar | Growing business, multiple cashiers | Multi-user with roles, reports, electronic invoicing, cloud backup |
| Avanzado / Multisucursal | Multi-location businesses | Multi-branch, remote dashboard, payment integrations, priority support |
| Implementación / Services | Any client needing customization | Custom development, integrations, training |

**Model:** Monthly subscription in Córdobas. Freemium or 15-30 day trial to reduce adoption friction.

## Go-to-market

1. **Pilot phase:** Install POS free or at symbolic cost in 5-10 nearby businesses
2. **Iterate:** Adjust product based on real problems detected
3. **Convert:** Turn pilot businesses into first paying customers
4. **Leverage:** Use service project revenue to fund product development while customer base grows
